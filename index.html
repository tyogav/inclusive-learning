01. Web Interventions

ReadEase

Visual Clutter Reduction Alternative Formatting

An assistive reading interface built to strip away visual distractions and present complex text structures in a highly digestible format.

Classroom Integration: Perfect for students who get overwhelmed by dense textbook pages. Project this on a smartboard during reading sessions or send the link to parents for distraction-free homework reading.

Addition Master

Low Cognitive Load Sensory Regulated UI

A gamified mathematics environment utilizing low-cognitive-load principles to help learners master foundational calculation.

Classroom Integration: Ideal for resource room pull-outs. It removes the anxiety of timed classroom math and allows students needing foundational numeracy support to practice at their own pace without sensory overload.

Pronounce Better

Articulation Support Self-Paced Practice

An interactive phonetics tool designed to lower the barrier for communication practice, offering guided, low-anxiety speech modules.

Classroom Integration: Use this as a warm-up activity for students requiring speech and language reinforcement before asking them to read aloud in front of peers.

02. Auditory Pedagogy

10th Std. Audio Repository

Auditory Reinforcement Information Chunking

A comprehensive repository of 1-mark question audio lessons, curated for diverse learners in Tamil Medium.

Why this works (The Dual-Registration Method)

These assets use explicit semantic labeling and double-repetition of Q&A pairs, concluding with concise keyword summaries. This structure is proven to cement long-term cognitive retention better than standard read-alouds.

Classroom Integration: Share this Drive folder link directly in your Class 10 WhatsApp groups for exam preparation. Highly recommended for late bloomers and auditory learners to listen to repeatedly before board exams.
